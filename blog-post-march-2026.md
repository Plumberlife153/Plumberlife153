# 5 Signs You Need to Call a Plumber Before It's Too Late

---

Spring is here, and it's the perfect time to make sure your plumbing is in top shape. Many homeowners ignore small plumbing issues until they become expensive emergencies. Here are 5 warning signs you shouldn't ignore:

## 1. Slow Drains

A slow drain might seem minor, but it often signals a bigger blockage building up in your pipes. If multiple drains in your home are slow, you could be looking at a main sewer line issue.

## 2. Water Stains on Walls or Ceilings

Brown or yellowish stains on your walls or ceiling are a red flag for hidden leaks. Left untreated, these leaks can cause mold growth and serious structural damage.

## 3. Low Water Pressure

If your water pressure has suddenly dropped, it could mean corroded pipes, a hidden leak, or a problem with your water line. Don't just live with it — get it checked out.

## 4. Running Toilets

A toilet that won't stop running can waste hundreds of gallons of water a month, driving up your water bill. Usually it's a simple fix, but it needs professional attention.

## 5. Strange Sounds in Your Pipes

Banging, gurgling, or whistling sounds from your pipes are not normal. These noises can indicate air in the lines, water hammer, or even pipe damage.
