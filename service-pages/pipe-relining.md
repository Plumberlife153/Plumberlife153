## What is Pipe Re-Lining?

Pipe relining (also called CIPP — cured-in-place pipe) uses a resin-saturated liner that's inserted into your existing damaged pipe. Once in place, the liner hardens to create a **new pipe within the old one** — seamless, durable, and corrosion-resistant.

The result: a pipe that's often **stronger than the original** and expected to last 50+ years.

## Our Pipe Relining Process

1. **Video inspection** to confirm relining is appropriate
2. **Thorough cleaning** (usually sewer jetting) to prep the pipe
3. **Measure and cut** the liner to exact length
4. **Saturate the liner** with epoxy resin
5. **Insert and inflate** the liner inside your existing pipe
6. **Cure the resin** to form a new, rigid pipe
7. **Final camera inspection** to verify success
