## What is Sewer Jetting?

Sewer jetting uses a specialized hose with a high-pressure nozzle that propels water through your sewer lines at thousands of PSI. This pressurized water:

- **Cuts through grease** that coats the pipe walls
- **Breaks up mineral scale** and mud buildup
- **Clears tree roots** that have invaded the line
- **Flushes out debris** completely — not just pushing it further
- **Restores pipes** to near-original flow capacity

Unlike snaking (which just punches a hole through a clog), jetting **scrubs the entire pipe clean** from wall to wall.

## Signs You Need Sewer Jetting

- Multiple drains in your home are slow or backing up
- You've had the same clog cleared multiple times
- Gurgling sounds from toilets or drains
- Sewage smell in your yard or basement
- Water backs up in one drain when you use another
- Old home with cast iron or clay sewer pipes
- Trees or large shrubs planted near the sewer line
