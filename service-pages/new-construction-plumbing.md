## Our New Construction Process

### 1. Planning & Quoting
- Review blueprints or plans
- Provide a detailed written estimate
- Identify any issues before work starts

### 2. Permitting
- Pull required permits with the municipality
- Coordinate with building officials

### 3. Underground & Slab Work
- Sewer and water line installation
- Under-slab rough-in before concrete

### 4. Rough-In Plumbing
- Install water supply, drain, waste, vent lines
- Position fixtures for final installation
- Pass rough-in inspection

### 5. Final Installation
- Set toilets, sinks, tubs, showers
- Install faucets and fixtures
- Connect water heaters and appliances
- Pressure testing and final checks

### 6. Final Inspection & Turnover
- Pass final municipal inspection
- Walk-through with builder or homeowner
- Provide warranty and documentation
