## What We Look For

Our cameras can identify:

- **Blockages** — grease, roots, foreign objects
- **Cracked or broken pipes**
- **Bellied or sagging sections** where water collects
- **Offset joints** where sections have shifted
- **Root intrusion** — exactly where it's entering
- **Corroded pipes** — especially in older homes
- **Pipe material** — cast iron, clay, PVC, Orangeburg
- **Pipe diameter and condition**
