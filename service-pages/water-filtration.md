## Types of Water Filtration We Install

### Whole-Home Carbon Filters
Remove chlorine, chloramine, bad tastes, and odors from every tap in your home. Great for city water customers in Rock Hill and Fort Mill.

### Water Softeners
Eliminate hard water by removing calcium and magnesium. Protects your plumbing, appliances, and skin. Essential for well water users.

### Reverse Osmosis (RO) Systems
Typically installed under the kitchen sink for ultra-pure drinking water. Removes 99% of dissolved contaminants.

### Sediment Filters
Catch sand, silt, rust, and debris — especially important for homes on well water.

### UV Purification
Kills bacteria, viruses, and pathogens. Recommended for well water or where microbial contamination is a concern.

### Iron & Sulfur Removal
Specialized systems for well water with high iron (rust stains) or sulfur (rotten egg smell).
